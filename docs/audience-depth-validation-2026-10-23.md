# Audience depth after owner feedback

The owner found the revised language better but asked for substantial differences
in length, assumed knowledge and explanation. This round changes selection and
depth as well as voice. Tali 0.3.0 records that distinction in its mutation,
review and publication instructions.

The source and complete fact skeleton remain unchanged. Coverage contracts were
pinned before writing. The two summaries intentionally defer implementation
catalogs and the full results table, with links to the detailed edition. Their
coverage maps record what was retained, condensed or deferred and why.

Independent agents reviewed every edition against its contract and source.
The beginner review caught wording that could imply delivery to a client; that
implication was removed. Both summaries received further jargon simplification.
Rechecks passed essential coverage, included assertions, qualifiers and
misleading-omission review. These are agent assessments, not human comprehension
tests or owner acceptance.

The technical edition passed independent review of all 95 claims and 21
distinctions. Its Markdown and extracted PDF text each passed the unchanged
147-item mechanical fidelity check. The summaries were not presented as passing
that full-coverage check. Added explanations were checked for support and were
not treated as new implementation results.

All seven PDF pages were rendered and visually inspected. Each edition's source
words survived PDF extraction in order. Five HTML pages passed link, navigation,
desktop and mobile width checks without JavaScript errors. De-tell lint found
zero strip findings and three documented, source-supported review findings.
The repository's 71 tests passed.
