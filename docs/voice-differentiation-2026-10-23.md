# Voice differentiation after owner feedback

The owner found the first four-version comparison practically identical.
Fidelity checks had passed, but the voice profiles were too similar and there
was no separate check of whether readers could distinguish the voices. The
second-campaign step was reopened. When asked to choose tones, the owner
delegated the decision to the agent.

All three copies were regenerated as conversational explanation, formal
committee memorandum and terse engineering notes. They use different sentence
structures and ways of addressing the reader. The source, frozen skeleton,
metrics table and all 147 mechanical requirements remained unchanged. The
previously accepted plain copy remains in private history; acceptance was not
transferred to its replacement.

Three independent semantic reviewers checked all 95 claims and 21 distinctions.
One correction removed an invented ordering requirement for committee decisions;
only the roadmap was prioritized in the source. The correction was independently
rechecked. Final fidelity passed all three copies, with zero strip findings and
seven documented review findings. No waivers or unresolved findings remain.

A fourth reviewer received anonymous copies without voice profiles or intended
labels. It correctly identified all three styles and found sustained differences
across the opening, pipeline, evidence, limitations and requests. Its verdict was
pass with a reservation: conversational and formal remain the closest pair,
though distinguishable. This is an independent agent assessment, not owner
acceptance or a claim of radically different authorial personalities.

The review entry now shows the same opening side by side. Full comparison pages,
redlines, change summaries and four PDFs were rebuilt. Browser navigation,
desktop/mobile width and entry links passed. All 25 PDF pages were rendered and
visually inspected; all four extracted PDF texts passed 147 fidelity items.
Publication layout corrections did not change the factual text.

The campaign repository has no remote; only sanitized evidence and board
metadata are published. The revised round is complete, and no further tone
