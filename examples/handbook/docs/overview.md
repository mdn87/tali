# Team tools handbook
**Status:** example
**Owner:** platform team

This handbook covers the tools the team runs every day: the sync agent, the command-line tool (CLI), and the desktop app. It is written for two audiences, new hires and engineers, and each audience gets its own voice of the same facts.

## Support tiers

Every request to the platform team is assigned exactly one of three support tiers when it is opened:

- **Tier 1**: the team responds within one business day. Covers outages and anything that blocks a release.
- **Tier 2**: the team responds within three business days. Covers degraded behavior with a workaround.
- **Tier 3**: the team responds within ten business days. Covers questions, requests for new features, and documentation gaps.

The tier is set by the person who triages the request, not by the person who opens it. A request can move between tiers once triage has looked at it.

## How to read this handbook

**Recommended.** Read the setup guide first, then the data-handling policy. The policy is short and it is the one document everyone is expected to know.

The glossary in the reference section is kept as a table and is identical in every voice; it is the one place where wording never changes.
